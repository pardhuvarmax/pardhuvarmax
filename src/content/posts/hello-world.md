This is a place for working notes: things I am reading, building, and trying to understand properly before I trust them.

## What to expect

- **Systems security.** Linux internals, kernel-level defense, eBPF, and the occasional rabbit hole through source code.
- **Learned systems after deployment.** How machine learning models adapt, drift, and what it takes to keep their behaviour controllable.
- **Tooling.** Small, terminal-first developer tools, mostly in Rust and C.

## How I write

Short, specific, and honest about what I do not know yet. If a note turns into a result, it becomes a paper; the [publications](/#publications) section tracks those.
